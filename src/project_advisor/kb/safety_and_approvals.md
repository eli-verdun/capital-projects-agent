# Safety And Approvals

Synthetic Meridian Project Delivery Advisor demo, snapshot 2026-10-29.

The advisor is read-only. It cannot accept tests, waive or close hold points, close technical queries or nonconformances, approve change orders or purchase orders, release for shipment, sign mechanical completion or handover certificates, issue permits, change trip settings, or start or stop equipment. Do not present generic steps as a project-approved procedure. Refer to the approved project procedure and authorized staff.
