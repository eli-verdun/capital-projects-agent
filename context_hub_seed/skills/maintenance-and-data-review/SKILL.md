# Review maintenance due dates and the quality of project observations.

Use get_package, get_maintenance_schedule and query_quality_records. Compare BOTH run-hour and calendar limits against the fixed snapshot. Use get_project_snapshot to check as_of and quality; stale data cannot confirm current readiness. Equipment status cannot establish mechanical completion, handover or performance acceptance.
